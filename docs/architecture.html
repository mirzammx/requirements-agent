How a tender becomes a reply to BD

Outlook rules bring the mail in. The AI sorts the BidAssist list and writes a summary of each RFQ, Mahadevan decides whether to bid, and the AI drafts the reply documents. The reply to BD waits in your Outlook Drafts. The AI never sends, never prices and never decides.

2Inside one AI step, and where the rules are enforced

Sorting the list, the AI summary and the reply documents all follow the same pattern. The model does one thing, writing structured content, and ordinary code checks it and lays it out. That is why the documents look the same every time and why the safety rules hold even if the model gets something wrong.

4What each part guarantees

Nothing is sent by itself

Replies to BD are saved in Outlook Drafts. A person checks each one and presses Send.

The right tender, checked

A GeM document is only used if the bid number printed inside it matches the tender.

No tender is dropped

If the AI misses a row or a batch fails, the tender goes to "AI wasn't sure" for a person to look at.

No prices from the AI

The draft has no price field. Price cells in Price_Bid.xlsx stay blank and yellow; totals and GST are formulas.

No claims without proof

AS9100, CEMILAC, Udyam or vendor registration is only stated if company_profile.toml records it.

Every gap is visible

Anything a person must decide is a yellow gap, and each document opens with its count.

Every fact has a source

The AI summary cites the file and page, so a reviewer can check a claim in seconds.

Paid work is never lost

The AI's draft is saved before documents are written; "Remake documents" reuses it with no new AI call.

5Still done by people, and what could come next

  • Other sites

    RFQs on IOCL, defproc or CPPP need a login there, so BD downloads and uploads them. GeM tenders are fetched automatically.

  • Sending

    Each reply to BD is checked and sent by a person from Outlook Drafts. This stays manual on purpose.

  • Price & submit

    Prices, signatures and the portal upload are done by people. This stays manual on purpose.

  • AI provider

    Testing runs on free Gemini, which is sometimes busy. Next: paid Claude, so tender documents aren't used by the provider and runs don't fail for busy servers.

  • Learning

    Outcomes aren't recorded yet. Next: log wins, losses and L1 prices to sharpen the sorting and give BD price history.
